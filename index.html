Journey

How I got here.

Early Days · The Spark
Fell in love with how things work
It started with taking things apart. Remotes, motors, broken gadgets — not to fix them, but to understand what was inside. The question was always the same: "But why does this work?" That obsessive curiosity became the foundation of everything.
Engineering · The Foundation
ECE — where logic became craft
Enrolled in Electronics & Communication Engineering. Discovered that circuits speak a language more precise than any other. Semiconductor physics, signal theory, communication systems — not just textbook chapters, but real explanations for how our modern world runs on invisible signals.
Building · First Experiments
Laser beams and light through glass
Built the first real projects — a laser voice transmission system, an optical fiber communication link, and a demonstration that made light literally bend around water. Each build was a lesson: theory is only as good as what you can make with your hands.
Exploring · The Pivot
AI, IoT, and the art of storytelling
Realized that great technology without great communication disappears. Started exploring AI and IoT — the convergence layers of the future. Simultaneously developed an eye for cinematics and visual storytelling. The engineer met the creator. The result? Someone who can build it and make you feel it.
Now · The Build
Building the future, one system at a time
Currently deep in AI research and IoT systems, while building a creative portfolio that bridges both worlds. Every day is a new proof of concept. The goal isn't to find a job in the industry — it's to define a new lane within it.
